## Prompt (English)

Create one original 15-second vertical 9:16 realistic smartphone fashion short, using the five uploaded storyboard frames in their exact order. Preserve the exact same adult Chinese woman from the reference frames: identical face shape, facial feature proportions, skin tone, age impression, body proportions, long black hair styled in two low buns, and the small matte blue hair clip on the left side. She must remain the same single person in every shot. Warm, lived-in apartment lighting, gentle phone-camera compression, subtle sensor grain, slight autofocus breathing and minor exposure variation; natural skin texture and flyaway hairs, never beauty-filtered or glossy AI skin.

The woman is barefoot throughout the entire video. In all full-body shots, show natural bare feet contacting the floor/rug with correct weight and soft contact shadows. Absolutely no shoes, socks, slippers, heels, boots, sandals, or footwear of any kind; no footwear may appear in the room.

0.0–2.0s — Fixed full-body smartphone framing in the warm living room. She wears the ivory pajama set with tiny blue floral print from reference 1, stands barefoot on the rug, looks gently playful and makes two small finger-warm-up motions toward the camera. Keep the room, camera height, perspective and lighting stable.

2.0–4.5s — Still in the same position and same fixed frame. She touches her thumb to her index finger, then to her middle finger. A small stream of clean white pixel squares rises from the floor and briefly covers her torso; exactly as the pixels clear, transform her continuously into the mint sleeveless knit top and pale-yellow ruffled mini skirt in reference 2. Her bare feet never move unnaturally; she makes a tiny natural half-turn after the change. One short soft pixel-whoosh and a light beat accent.

4.5–7.0s — The same room and continuous body balance. She snaps once with her right hand; the snap is the only transition trigger. At that instant, transform cleanly into the pink-and-white striped long-sleeve top and grey-white layered mini skirt in reference 3, still barefoot. Add a tiny natural camera push-in only; she finishes with a small hand-heart gesture. One subtle snap-pop and fabric rustle.

7.0–10.0s — She sweeps her left hand upward. A single blue-and-white checked cloth crosses close to the lens as an intentional practical wipe. Only behind that cloth wipe, transform into the grey-blue sleeveless vest and navy pleated skirt from reference 4. She takes one balanced step toward the camera and settles naturally, barefoot, with realistic foot-to-floor contact. Use one soft cloth-swish and beat accent.

10.0–12.5s — Cut once to a warm dining-table close-up. Same woman, same final grey-blue vest and navy pleated skirt. She lightly taps a fork against a strawberry cake, glances upward and smiles naturally. Keep it ordinary and intimate, with realistic tabletop lighting.

12.5–15.0s — Cut to a close smartphone-front-camera feeling. Same final look, same hair and face. She blinks once, then gently taps the lens with one index finger. Hold the final expression for the last 0.5 seconds.

Use original non-lyrical sound design only: fingertip taps, soft pixel whoosh, one snap pop, cloth swish, quiet fork-on-plate sound, and restrained beat accents. Do not imitate, quote, or reproduce any existing creator, soundtrack, clip, text, or specific scene.

## Hard negative constraints

- Never change her identity, face, body, age, hairstyle, blue hair clip, or skin tone.
- Exactly one person only. No duplicate, reflection-person, background people, face replacement, face drift, body morphing or beauty-doll look.
- No footwear of any kind: no shoes, socks, slippers, heels, boots, sandals, bare-foot-shaped shoes, or footwear appearing in the environment.
- No extra limbs, malformed hands, extra fingers, detached limbs, floating body, broken perspective, or foot sliding.
- Do not layer outfits. Each new outfit completely replaces the prior outfit only during its specified occlusion: pixel squares, hand-snap moment, or checked-cloth wipe.
- No abrupt background jump, light-direction change, camera teleport, unreadable text, subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks, UI, or gibberish. Add any title, arrows, hearts, or end text later in post-production only.
