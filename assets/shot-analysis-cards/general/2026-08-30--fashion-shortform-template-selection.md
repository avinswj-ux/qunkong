# Fashion short-form template selection

## 已验证结论

- One short video should have one primary objective: fast saves, comments, mood-led seeding, or styling knowledge.
- Fixed setting, camera, full-body framing, and one durable visual anchor improve outfit legibility more than complex scene changes.
- A product or audience benefit should be readable in the first three seconds. Keep CTA concise and do not cover face or garment.
- Four-look videos work especially well as dynamic lookbook units: one simple grounded motion per look, with clothing and colour as the primary changing variables.

## 适用条件

Use a dynamic lookbook for four supplied looks when the creator wants a concise, product-visible outfit transformation. Use an interactive vote structure only when the final edit will include clear numbered choice stickers and a comment CTA.


## 生成 / 分镜硬规则

- Use one scene and one camera position.
- Preserve a full-body view and one persistent accessory or framing anchor.
- Use a single motion per look and one action-caused cover for each transition.
- Reserve any post-production knowledge card for the final edit; do not ask the image/video model to render tiny text.
